PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 25 September 2026

FMGS-A320 (the “app”) is published by ADDIE OÜ, Estonia (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

NO DATA COLLECTED

The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours: all functions work offline on your device.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

  • The FMS standard you select (Thales or Honeywell) is saved on your device so that it is offered again at the next start.
  • Flight plans, weights, winds and other data you type into the trainer are held in memory only while the app runs.

Deleting the app removes this information from your device.

SUBSCRIPTIONS AND PAYMENTS

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app checks your subscription status on your device with Apple’s StoreKit. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

LINKS

Links on this page that lead to other websites open outside the app; those websites have their own privacy practices.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTACT

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, contact ADDIE OÜ at info@addieproject.eu. You may also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
